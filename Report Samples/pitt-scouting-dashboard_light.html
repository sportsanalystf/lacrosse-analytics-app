EXPECTED GOALS (xG) MODEL — PITTSBURGH SHOT QUALITY

Zone-based xG using NCAA D1 average conversion rates · FAE = Finishing Above Expected (Goals − ΣxG)

MODEL METHODOLOGY

xG assigns a probability of scoring to every shot based on where it was taken from. NCAA D1 women's lacrosse baseline conversion rates: Crease = 0.58, 8m arc = 0.38, 12m+ = 0.18. Each shot is assigned its zone's baseline xG, and summed per player.

Finishing Above Expected (FAE) = Actual Goals − ΣxG. Positive FAE = elite finishing. Negative FAE = shooting below expectation. FAE tells you who is overperforming their shot quality — and who might regress.

GSAx — GOALS SAVED ABOVE EXPECTED (CAIN)

xSV model: Expected saves based on shot quality faced · GSAx = Actual Saves − Expected Saves

MODEL METHODOLOGY

xSV (Expected Save) assigns a save probability to each shot faced based on zone. NCAA D1 average save rates: Crease = 0.32, 8m = 0.42, 12m+ = 0.55. Each shot on goal is assigned its zone's baseline xSV, summed, then compared to actual saves.

GSAx = Actual Saves − Expected Saves. Positive GSAx = goalie is outperforming. Negative GSAx = underperforming.
